AN UNFILTERED FAMILY JOURNEY

"We Refused to Strip What Nature Gave Us"

BOVA Adventura was started in our home kitchen. We saw how commercial skincare brands bleached shea butter to remove its natural nutty scent, stripped away vital vitamins, and packed products with artificial perfumes that caused breakouts.

We went back to the traditional methods our grandmother taught us in Oyo State. Cold-pressing wild shea nuts, roasting plantain peels into rich black soap ash, and harvesting raw forest honey without heating.

When you hold a BOVA jar, you are holding the work of real Nigerian women, fair-trade farmers, and a family that truly cares about your skin's long-term health.
